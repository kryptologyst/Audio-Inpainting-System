# Audio Inpainting System - Privacy & Ethics Disclaimer

## Research and Educational Use Only

This audio inpainting system is designed exclusively for **research and educational purposes**. It is intended to advance the field of audio signal processing and restoration through academic study and learning.

## Prohibited Uses

**This system MUST NOT be used for:**

- **Biometric identification** or authentication systems
- **Voice cloning** or impersonation
- **Deepfake generation** or manipulation
- **Deceptive audio synthesis** for malicious purposes
- **Privacy violations** or unauthorized audio manipulation
- **Commercial applications** without proper ethical review
- **Any form of audio forgery** or fraud

## Ethical Guidelines

### For Researchers
- Use only for legitimate audio restoration research
- Ensure proper consent for any audio data used
- Follow institutional review board (IRB) guidelines
- Document all use cases and applications
- Respect privacy and data protection regulations

### For Educators
- Use only in controlled educational environments
- Emphasize ethical considerations in audio processing
- Teach responsible use of audio manipulation tools
- Discuss potential misuse scenarios with students

### For Developers
- Implement additional safeguards for production use
- Add watermarking or detection mechanisms
- Include usage logging and monitoring
- Regular ethical review of applications

## Technical Safeguards

This system includes several technical safeguards:

- **No biometric features**: Models do not extract speaker-specific characteristics
- **Research focus**: Optimized for audio restoration, not voice synthesis
- **Transparency**: Open-source code allows for audit and verification
- **Limited scope**: Designed for specific inpainting tasks only

## Legal Compliance

Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with:

- **Local privacy laws** (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- **Audio recording regulations** in their jurisdiction
- **Intellectual property rights** for any audio content
- **Institutional policies** for research and education
